#  SedaqahEase - Mosque Donation Management System (MDMS)

## Overview

SedaqahEase is a web-based Mosque Donation Management System (MDMS) developed to help mosques manage donation activities efficiently and transparently. The system was designed specifically for Masjid Bandar Saujana Putra, where donation records were previously maintained manually, making them difficult to organize and monitor.

By digitizing donation management, SedaqahEase enables mosque administrators to track contributions, manage campaigns, allocate funds, and generate reports with ease. The system improves transparency and accountability while providing donors with convenient access to their donation records.

---

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of SedaqahEase are:

* To digitalize mosque donation management processes.
* To provide a transparent platform for tracking donations.
* To simplify donation recording and fund allocation.
* To improve financial record management.
* To provide donors with easy access to their donation history.
* To generate detailed reports for better decision-making.

---

## User Roles

###  Donor

Donors can:

* Register and log into the system.
* Make online donations.
* Upload donation receipts.
* View donation history.
* Track contributions made to the mosque.

###  Treasurer

Treasurers can:

* Record online donations.
* Record physical donations collected through mosque donation boxes.
* Manage financial records.
* Allocate funds to mosque activities and programs.
* Monitor donation transactions.

###  Admin

Admins have full system access and can:

* Manage user accounts.
* Approve or reject donation campaigns.
* Monitor system activities.
* Generate comprehensive donation reports.
* Manage overall system operations.
